Questions about Claude video

Can Claude make videos?

Yes. Claude has no video model: it writes code that draws every frame, the way the Opus 5.5 animations people share on X are made. KitCut has Claude Opus 5.5 write the script, animate every scene, write a narration and compose the music, then renders it all into a finished Claude video.

How do I make a Claude video?

Type one sentence about your idea, pick a length and a look (hand-drawn, painted or collage), and press Make the film. Claude plans it, draws it, looks at stills of its own work and fixes what it doesn't like. The finished video is ready in a few minutes.

Which Claude model makes the videos?

Claude Opus 5.5 directs every video. It writes the script and the code that draws each frame, and reviews the result before it is rendered. For the painted look an image model paints the scenes and Claude animates them; for the collage look one paints pictures cut out of paper and Claude builds pages round them; a voice model reads Claude's narration.

Is it free?

Yes, to start: every account gets 30 seconds of film a month, with no card needed. Paid plans give you more minutes, and Pro makes films up to 8 minutes long.

How long can a Claude video be?

Up to 1 minute on Free and Standard, and 8 minutes on Pro. A month's free credits make one 30-second film.

What can I make with it?

Motion graphics, explainer videos, launch videos, tiny stories, music videos, showreels and lessons, in any language you type in. Every film gets its own page to share.
