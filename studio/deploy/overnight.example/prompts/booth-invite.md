A 30-second trade-show booth invitation: "Find us at RSNA 2026". It is the film an exhibitor posts before a show so visitors come to its stand.

The show: RSNA 2026, the Radiological Society of North America's annual meeting, November 29 to December 3, 2026, McCormick Place, Chicago. Technical Exhibits are open November 29 to December 2, 10 a.m. to 5 p.m. CT (rsna.org/annual-meeting).
The exhibitor: Radiology Partners, which publishes its RSNA 2026 page at https://www.radpartners.com/event/rsna-2026/ . Read that page (and RSNA's exhibitor list) for its booth number, hall and anything it will show there. If no booth number is published, say "at RSNA 2026" and leave the number off.

Beats:
1. "We're exhibiting": the show's name, dates and city, with the exhibitor's logo.
2. The hall as a simple drawn floor plan of stands (a generic grid: do not copy RSNA's real floor plan, and name no other exhibitor). The camera flies over it to one stand, which lights up in the exhibitor's colour with its hall and booth number.
3. The stand rises as a small booth with the exhibitor's logo on it, and up to three things to see there appear as signs, only if the exhibitor publishes them.
4. Exhibit hours, then the end card: the booth number large, the dates, the exhibitor's logo and its link.

How to make it (this film will become a template others remake for their own event):
- Use only facts published on the pages above or on the organiser's own pages. Never invent a date, time, address, price, name or number; a fact you cannot find is left out and the layout still holds.
- Use the real logos and the exhibitor's own colours and type (fetch them from its site). Draw nothing generic where the real thing exists. No mascots, no jokes, no theme costume: clean, brand-led, typographic motion design of the kind a good in-house designer would post.
- Keep every fact, colour and picture name in one `const FACTS = {...}` object at the top of film.js, and nothing of them in the code below it.
- Fit long values (shrink, then wrap); lists lay out for 1 to 4 items; an optional item that is missing leaves no hole.
- Lay everything out from the frame's width and height, not fixed pixels, so the same code holds in 16:9, 1:1 and 9:16.
- Narration: short, warm, plain. Say the day and time the way a person would. Never read a URL aloud.
- Readable on a phone: nothing important smaller than a caption.
