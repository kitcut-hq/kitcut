# Padel carries Corcuera's walls around the world

The best-supported account of padel's birth is a walled court of about 20 × 10 m. Enrique Corcuera, a Mexican businessman, built it at his house in Las Brisas, Acapulco, in 1969. It borrowed the short wooden bat of American paddle tennis, which Frank Peer Beal devised and Wikipedia dates to 1898, and in effect the playable enclosure of platform tennis (Scarsdale, New York, 1928). The difference was solid walls where those games had nothing or wire. Prince Alfonso de Hohenlohe converted a tennis court at his Marbella Club in 1974. An Argentine, usually named as Julio Menditeguy, took the game home in the mid-1970s. In the 1990s Argentina turned it into a mass craze, which the 2001 economic crisis broke. Spain inherited Argentina's players and became the sport's heartland, and northern Europe, the Gulf and Britain followed from the late 2010s. Argentina, Spain and Uruguay founded the International Padel Federation (FIP) in Madrid on 12 July 1991. The FIP staged the first World Championship in 1992 and had 115 member federations on 12 October 2026. The professional game passed through three tours in turn:

- the Padel Pro Tour (2006–2012)
- the World Padel Tour (2013–2023)
- Premier Padel, governed by the FIP and backed by Qatar, which absorbed its rival after Qatar Sports Investments agreed to buy WPT on 24 August 2023

The court's size is the one thing that has not changed since 1969. Walls went from concrete to glass and rackets from wood to carbon. The deuce rule went from advantage to the golden point to the 2026 Star Point. How big padel is now depends on who is counting, and neither main count is a census. The FIP's October 2026 report claims 91,234 courts, 28,497 clubs and more than 40 million players in 191 countries and territories. The Playtomic/PwC Strategy& report counted 58,334 club courts and 19.4 million players at the end of 2025. Much of the founding story comes from people with a stake in it: the Corcuera family, the Marbella Club and the FIP. Three questions remain disputed: the date (the 1950s, 1962, 1968 or 1969), who wrote the first rules, and which Menditeguy brother reached Argentina. The film is most credible when it says so.

## Paddle tennis gave padel its bat; platform tennis made the enclosure part of play

Padel's oldest identifiable ancestor is **paddle tennis**, devised by the Episcopal minister **Frank Peer Beal** in **Albion, Michigan**. It was played on a court of 18 × 39 ft, exactly half a tennis court. Wikipedia dates it to **1898** ([Wikipedia, Paddle tennis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paddle_tennis)), and Padder Tennis New Zealand dates its own game's lineage to the same year ([Padder Tennis NZ](https://www.paddertennis.nz/)) [SS for 1898]. Britannica confirms that Beal invented the game as a child in Albion and, as a New York City official, introduced it to the city's playgrounds in the early 1920s ([Britannica, Frank P. Beal](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Frank-P-Beal)) [WS for Beal and Albion]. Beal had courts laid in **Washington Square Park in 1915**; the first tournament followed in **1922** and the US Paddle Tennis Association in **1923** ([Wikipedia, Paddle tennis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paddle_tennis)) [DW, Wikipedia only].

Platform tennis contributed a rule rather than a wall. In autumn **1928** two neighbours, **Fessenden Blanchard and James Cogswell**, built a 48 × 20 ft wooden platform on Cogswell's land in **Scarsdale, New York**. They bought paddles and sponge-rubber balls from a local store and put up chicken wire because they were tired of digging balls out of snowbanks. They then agreed that a ball could be played off the screens after it had bounced in the court ([Platform Tennis Hall of Fame](https://platformtennishalloffame.org/history-of-the-game/); [Britannica, Platform tennis](https://www.britannica.com/sports/platform-tennis)) [WS]. By the end of 1932 Scarsdale had seven platforms and held the first open tournament ([Platform Tennis Hall of Fame](https://platformtennishalloffame.org/history-of-the-game/)) [SS].

Padel combines paddle tennis's short solid bat and small court with an enclosure that is part of play, and makes that enclosure solid. That lineage is this report's reading of the two games' descriptions. The sources link Corcuera to paddle tennis: his household eventually played with wooden paddles made for American paddle tennis ([Minter Dial, 2021](https://www.minterdial.com/2021/06/padel-tennis-courts-world/)). No source shows that he knew platform tennis.

Britain had a cousin of its own. A British Pathé newsreel issued on **30 April 1951** shows **Dan Maskell** teaching "the new game of Padder Tennis" in Ruislip ([British Pathé](https://www.britishpathe.com/asset/82961/)) [WS, primary film]. New Zealand still plays padder tennis as its version of paddle tennis, on a 15 × 6 m court ([Padder Tennis NZ](https://www.paddertennis.nz/)). The much-repeated claim that a similar game was played on **19th-century British cruise ships** appears on LTA and Red Bull pages with no primary evidence behind it ([LTA Padel](https://www.ltapadel.org.uk/what-we-do/what-is-the-history-of-padel/); [Red Bull](https://www.redbull.com/gb-en/history-of-padel)) [DW]. The documented shipboard game is deck tennis, and the first Scarsdale platform was itself built for deck tennis and badminton ([Platform Tennis Hall of Fame](https://platformtennishalloffame.org/history-of-the-game/)).

The name came later than the game. The Real Academia Española derives **pádel** from the English "paddle tennis" and recommends it over "paddle" ([DLE](https://dle.rae.es/p%C3%A1del); [RAE, DPD](https://www.rae.es/dpd/p%C3%A1del)) [WS]. **P.A.D.E.L.** stands for "Promoción de Actividades Deportivas, Educativas y Lúdicas" and was an administrative device. The Spanish association founded in 1991 could not register a sport that was not yet recognised, so it registered under the acronym ([FIP (ES), Oct 2024](https://www.padelfip.com/es/2024/10/de-argentina-y-espana-a-la-fama-mundial-el-fenomeno-del-padel-que-conquisto-el-mundo/); [Martín Echegaray](https://es.linkedin.com/pulse/p%C3%A1del-se-escribe-con-e%C3%B1e-de-momento-mart%C3%ADn-echegaray-vales)) [WS]. Earlier names were:

- "Paddle Corcuera" ([LTA Padel](https://www.ltapadel.org.uk/what-we-do/what-is-the-history-of-padel/))
- "Pádel Corcuera" ([El Tiempo MX](https://eltiempomx.com/noticia/2022/padel-el-deporte-que-surgio-en-acapulco-gracias-a-la-flojera-de-enrique-corcuera.html))
- Hohenlohe's "padel tennis" ([Marbella Club](https://www.marbellaclub.com/blog/marbella-club-anniversary-trivia))
- "Rebotenis", a friend's suggestion ([La Nación, 2026](https://www.lanacion.com.ar/lifestyle/enrique-y-viviana-corcuera-el-magnate-mexicano-que-invento-el-padel-y-la-miss-argentina-que-escribio-nid01102026/))
- "paddle", still the common spelling in Argentina ([La Capital MdP](https://www.lacapitalmdp.com/historia-de-la-evolucion-del-paddle-en-argentina/))

Argentines and Spaniards agreed on "pádel" as the official name in **1997** ([La Nación, 2026](https://www.lanacion.com.ar/lifestyle/enrique-y-viviana-corcuera-el-magnate-mexicano-que-invento-el-padel-y-la-miss-argentina-que-escribio-nid01102026/)). Padel Magazine puts the unification of rules and name in Barcelona that year ([Padel Magazine](https://padel-magazine.co.uk/from-paddle-to-padel/)) [WS for 1997; SS for Barcelona].

## A walled garden court in Las Brisas, 1969, is the best-supported birth

The FIP, the LTA and La Nación agree on the core story. In **1969**, **Enrique Corcuera García Pimentel** had no room for a tennis court at his house in **Las Brisas, Acapulco**. Instead he built an enclosed court of **20 × 10 m** with **3 m walls** at the ends, metal mesh and a tennis net across the middle ([FIP, History](https://www.padelfip.com/history/); [LTA Padel](https://www.ltapadel.org.uk/what-we-do/what-is-the-history-of-padel/); [La Nación](https://www.lanacion.com.ar/deportes/el-extrano-invento-de-un-millonario-nid465796/)) [WS].

La Nación narrows the date to **October 1969**. It describes "a firm surface 10 metres wide by 20 long, with the novelty of walls at both ends, three metres high" ([La Nación](https://www.lanacion.com.ar/deportes/el-extrano-invento-de-un-millonario-nid465796/)) [SS]. One end of the court rested against the wall of the house ([La Nación, 2026](https://www.lanacion.com.ar/lifestyle/enrique-y-viviana-corcuera-el-magnate-mexicano-que-invento-el-padel-y-la-miss-argentina-que-escribio-nid01102026/)) [SS]. He called the game **"Paddle Corcuera"** ([LTA Padel](https://www.ltapadel.org.uk/what-we-do/what-is-the-history-of-padel/); [Minter Dial, 2021](https://www.minterdial.com/2021/06/padel-tennis-courts-world/)) [WS].

No plan, dated photograph, permit or 1969 press clipping has been found. The 20 × 10 m figure may be today's standard projected backwards. Nallé Grinda, who played in Acapulco as a child, says "each house had its rules" and that court lengths varied between 18 and 20 m ([Padel Magazine, Grinda](https://padel-magazine.co.uk/nalle-grinda-every-house-had-its-rules-in-acapulco/)) [SS].

The tellers give different reasons for the walls, though their versions fit together:

- The federations say a sloping lot left no room for a full tennis court ([FIP](https://www.padelfip.com/history/)).
- La Nación adds dense tropical vegetation and balls rolling down the slope ([La Nación, 2022](https://www.lanacion.com.ar/sabado/fue-boom-en-los-90-la-segunda-vida-del-deporte-que-marco-una-epoca-nid21012022/)).
- Mexican papers call it laziness: he would not chase balls ([El Tiempo MX](https://eltiempomx.com/noticia/2022/padel-el-deporte-que-surgio-en-acapulco-gracias-a-la-flojera-de-enrique-corcuera.html); [ESTO](https://esto.com.mx/602045-padel-el-deporte-que-surgio-en-acapulco-gracias-a-la-flojera-de-enrique-corcuera/)).
- Viviana Corcuera has told a siesta story, in which her hitting against a wall disturbed his nap ([Padel Magazine](https://padel-magazine.co.uk/viviana-corcuera-hubby-is-the-inventor-of-padel/)).
- She has also quoted him: "let's make a closed court because I'm not going to go looking for the ball" ([Minter Dial, 2025](https://www.minterdial.com/2025/05/rackets-walls-and-passion-viviana-corcuera-reveals-the-origins-of-padel-audio-in-spanish/)).

These are compatible anecdotes from different tellers [DW]. The safest narration is what they share: no room, a slope, and walls so that nobody had to fetch the ball.

The year itself is contested, mostly by the family:

- His widow says **1968**: "I say it all started in '68" ([Minter Dial, 2025](https://www.minterdial.com/2025/05/rackets-walls-and-passion-viviana-corcuera-reveals-the-origins-of-padel-audio-in-spanish/)). English Wikipedia's infobox repeats it ([Wikipedia, Padel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Padel)).
- A "legend" reported by La Nación and repeated by Spanish Wikipedia gives **1962**, when Corcuera added a wall to the frontón (a court for Basque pelota) at his Acapulco house ([La Nación, 2022](https://www.lanacion.com.ar/sabado/fue-boom-en-los-90-la-segunda-vida-del-deporte-que-marco-una-epoca-nid21012022/); [Wikipedia (es), Pádel](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/P%C3%A1del)).
- His son, **Enrique Corcuera Jr.**, traces the idea further back, to the **mid-1950s**. The family's sugar-cane hacienda at **Estipac, Jalisco**, had a frontón with a net strung across it ([Minter Dial, 2024](https://www.minterdial.com/2024/02/enrique-corcuera/); [Padel Magazine, fact-check](https://padel-magazine.co.uk/The-program-%22Watch-your-step%22-was-mistaken-about-the-origin-of-the-padel--we-checked/)).

All three are [DW]. A defensible line is: "In 1969, the date the federation and most newspapers give, Corcuera built a walled court at Las Brisas. His widow remembers 1968, and his son traces the idea to a frontón on the family hacienda in Jalisco in the 1950s."

**Viviana Corcuera** was born **Viviana Rosa Dellavedova** in Firmat, Argentina. She was crowned Miss Argentina on 25 July 1964, aged nineteen ([El Correo de Firmat](https://elcorreodigital.com.ar/hace-60-anos-una-firmatense-era-elegida-miss-argentina/)) [SS, local press], and met Corcuera in Mexico in 1968 ([La Nación, 2026](https://www.lanacion.com.ar/lifestyle/enrique-y-viviana-corcuera-el-magnate-mexicano-que-invento-el-padel-y-la-miss-argentina-que-escribio-nid01102026/)) [SS].

Who wrote the first rules is unresolved [DW]:

- **Enrique** is credited with a pamphlet titled "PADDLE CORCUERA" ([Minter Dial, 2021](https://www.minterdial.com/2021/06/padel-tennis-courts-world/)) and with "The Corcuera Rules" ([Marbella Club](https://www.marbellaclub.com/blog/marbella-club-anniversary-trivia)).
- **Viviana** says she "sat down, measured, and wrote a little booklet" as a birthday present for him ([Padel Magazine](https://padel-magazine.co.uk/viviana-corcuera-hubby-is-the-inventor-of-padel/)). The booklet is dated 1969 by Padel Magazine, to "the 1980s" in her own recollection ([La Nación, 2026](https://www.lanacion.com.ar/lifestyle/enrique-y-viviana-corcuera-el-magnate-mexicano-que-invento-el-padel-y-la-miss-argentina-que-escribio-nid01102026/)), and to a notebook in 1985 by a Mexican paper ([El Tiempo MX](https://eltiempomx.com/noticia/2022/padel-el-deporte-que-surgio-en-acapulco-gracias-a-la-flojera-de-enrique-corcuera.html)).
- Their son partly disputes her account ([Padel Magazine, fact-check](https://padel-magazine.co.uk/The-program-%22Watch-your-step%22-was-mistaken-about-the-origin-of-the-padel--we-checked/)).

No copy of either document has been located.

The first equipment was improvised: cut-down tennis rackets, then wooden paddles made for American paddle tennis ([Minter Dial, 2021](https://www.minterdial.com/2021/06/padel-tennis-courts-world/)). Viviana remembers "a very, very thin wooden racket… with little holes" ([Minter Dial, 2025](https://www.minterdial.com/2025/05/rackets-walls-and-passion-viviana-corcuera-reveals-the-origins-of-padel-audio-in-spanish/)). Every account mentions ordinary tennis balls ([Marbella Club](https://www.marbellaclub.com/blog/marbella-club-anniversary-trivia); [Minter Dial, 2024](https://www.minterdial.com/2024/02/enrique-corcuera/)) [WS for wooden paddles and tennis balls].

Corcuera died in **1999** ([Padel Magazine](https://padel-magazine.co.uk/viviana-corcuera-hubby-is-the-inventor-of-padel/); [Wikipedia (es)](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enrique_Corcuera_Garc%C3%ADa_y_Pimentel)) [SS]. Viviana then sold the house, and the new owners demolished the court to build a swimming pool ([Padel Magazine](https://padel-magazine.co.uk/viviana-corcuera-hubby-is-the-inventor-of-padel/)) [SS, family testimony]. Recognition came late. In **June 2022** the Mexican Senate gave Corcuera a posthumous recognition and recognised padel as a sport of Mexican origin; Viviana received it ([Cluster Pádel](https://www.clusterpadel.com/noticias-padel/20220617/padel-reconocido-oficialmente-como-deporte-senado-mexico.aspx); [Revista Clase](https://www.revistaclase.mx/eventos/senado-otorga-reconocimiento-postumo-enrique-corcuera/)) [WS]. In **December 2025** the municipality of Acapulco named the port the "cuna del pádel", the cradle of padel ([Gobierno de Acapulco](https://acapulco.gob.mx/2025/12/confirma-abelina-lopez-rodriguez-el-legado-de-acapulco-como-cuna-del-padel/)) [WS, official].

**What the sources let an illustrator draw in Acapulco** is spare: a firm surface on a steep, densely planted hillside lot, one end of the court against the house wall, walls about 3 m high at the ends, metal mesh, a tennis net, small wooden bats and tennis balls ([La Nación, 2022](https://www.lanacion.com.ar/sabado/fue-boom-en-los-90-la-segunda-vida-del-deporte-que-marco-una-epoca-nid21012022/); [Wikipedia (es)](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enrique_Corcuera_Garc%C3%ADa_y_Pimentel)). At one Acapulco house an air-conditioning unit on the court wall triggered a replay with two balls, and smashes kept landing in neighbours' gardens ([Padel Magazine, Grinda](https://padel-magazine.co.uk/nalle-grinda-every-house-had-its-rules-in-acapulco/)) [SS]. Early courts were cement slabs with no turf and no glass ([GIMPADEL](https://gimpadel.com/2018/10/26/evolucion-pistas-padel/); [Wikipedia, Padel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Padel)) [DW, but nothing contradicts it]. Glass, turf and blue surfaces therefore do not belong in a 1969 scene. No source describes what anyone wore, and no dated photograph of the first court was found. La Nación says the house could host Hohenlohe, Henry Kissinger and Frank Sinatra ([La Nación, 2026](https://www.lanacion.com.ar/lifestyle/enrique-y-viviana-corcuera-el-magnate-mexicano-que-invento-el-padel-y-la-miss-argentina-que-escribio-nid01102026/)) [SS], but nothing confirms that Kissinger or Sinatra played.

## Marbella and Argentina carried it abroad; Argentina's 1990s craze broke by 2001

The first court in Europe came out of a friendship. **Prince Alfonso de Hohenlohe-Langenburg** (Madrid 1924 – Marbella 2003) grew the Marbella Club out of his family's Finca Santa Margarita in 1954 ([Marbella Club, Our Story](https://www.marbellaclub.com/our-story); [Wikipedia (es), Hohenlohe](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfonso_de_Hohenlohe-Langenburg)). He learned the game at Corcuera's house. The hotel's own history quotes **Nicholas Foulkes**'s anniversary book: workers arrived at the club's tennis court one day in **1974**, "uprooted the net and erected cement walls at either end", and put chicken-wire mesh along the sides. Alfonso called it "padel tennis" ([Marbella Club, Anniversary Trivia](https://www.marbellaclub.com/blog/marbella-club-anniversary-trivia)) [WS: the hotel's account, the LTA ([LTA Padel](https://www.ltapadel.org.uk/what-we-do/what-is-the-history-of-padel/)) and both Wikipedias agree on 1974].

Several details remain uncertain or weakly sourced:

- The FIP says only "early 1970s", adding that Hohenlohe spent his summer holidays at Corcuera's Acapulco home ([FIP, History](https://www.padelfip.com/history/)) [SS]. A 1970 date appears only on blogs ([iPadel](https://ipadel.co.uk/History-of-Padel-Tennis)) [DW].
- It is unclear whether there were two courts or one. English Wikipedia says two ([Wikipedia, Padel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Padel)), while Foulkes describes one converted tennis court [DW].
- Many sources say Hohenlohe "changed the dimensions and rules", but none names a specific change ([padelrenting](https://www.padelrenting.com/blog/noticias-1/la-primera-pista-de-padel-en-espana-23)) [DW].

The original courts disappeared in a 1995 expansion of the club, leaving a bronze plaque set in a large rock ([Marbella Club](https://www.marbellaclub.com/blog/marbella-club-anniversary-trivia)) [SS]. The hotel's present "first padel court ever built in Europe" is a revival ([Marbella Club FAQ](https://www.marbellaclub.com/faq)). The tennis champion **Manolo Santana** is often said to have promoted the game on the Costa del Sol early on, but only weak sources say so ([Betway blog](https://blog.betway.es/padel/padel-en-espana-una-historia-de-amor-a-primera-vista/)) [DW]. His documented role in Marbella begins in 1983, when he took over the Puente Romano tennis club ([Wikipedia, Puente Romano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Club_de_Tenis_Puente_Romano)).

Argentina's arrival is credited to a **Menditeguy**, but accounts differ on which brother, by what route and when [DW on name and route; "mid-1970s" is the safe date]:

- The FIP says **Julio Menditeguy**, "an Argentine member of Alfonso's tennis club in Marbella", brought the sport home "around the same time" as Spain ([FIP, History](https://www.padelfip.com/history/)).
- Viviana, quoted by La Nación, says Julio visited Acapulco: "he took the measurements and features of the court and built it in Argentina. He was the first" ([La Nación, 2026](https://www.lanacion.com.ar/lifestyle/enrique-y-viviana-corcuera-el-magnate-mexicano-que-invento-el-padel-y-la-miss-argentina-que-escribio-nid01102026/)).
- The LTA dates the arrival to **1975** ([LTA Padel](https://www.ltapadel.org.uk/what-we-do/what-is-the-history-of-padel/)).
- Mar del Plata's La Capital credits his brother, **Carlos "Charly" Menditeguy** ([La Capital MdP](https://www.lacapitalmdp.com/historia-de-la-evolucion-del-paddle-en-argentina/)).

The first Argentine courts are named as the Menditeguy family's stud farm at San Antonio de Areco and the Blaquier family's estancia La Biznaga, followed by the Ocean Club in Mar del Plata ([La Nación](https://www.lanacion.com.ar/deportes/el-extrano-invento-de-un-millonario-nid465796/)) [SS]. By **1987** Argentina had 330 courts, 220 of them in Mar del Plata ([La Nación](https://www.lanacion.com.ar/lifestyle/enrique-y-viviana-corcuera-el-magnate-mexicano-que-invento-el-padel-y-la-miss-argentina-que-escribio-nid01102026/)) [SS].

Uruguay's documented start is a court at Balneario Zorba, Punta del Este, in summer **1983**, where the Argentine tennis players Ricardo Cano and Roberto "Cacho" Aubone drew holidaymakers ([Búsqueda](https://www.busqueda.com.uy/tiempo-libre/el-ave-fenix-los-deportes-el-padel-regreso-y-mas-fuerza-que-nunca-n5393642); [El Observador](https://www.elobservador.com.uy/nota/la-vuelta-del-padel-las-claves-que-explican-el-furor-por-el-deporte-insignia-de-los-90--20237305010)) [SS]. No tournament before 1980, in any country, was found with a name, date and winner.

In the 1990s padel became Argentina's mass craze and a symbol of the Menem years, "alongside the parripollo and the video club" ([Tiempo Argentino](https://www.tiempoar.com.ar/nota/redencion-presidencial-para-un-deporte-que-fue-boom-en-los-noventa); [DataClave](https://www.dataclave.com.ar/poder/el-padel--un-deporte-con-sello-menemista-que-regresa--que-politicos-juegan-_a6069d14e656b0833e6fbd4cb)). Nobody measured it, and every size estimate is unofficial [DW]:

- La Nación wrote of "more than 4 million players" and "more than 25,000 courts" in its years of glory ([La Nación, 2022](https://www.lanacion.com.ar/sabado/fue-boom-en-los-90-la-segunda-vida-del-deporte-que-marco-una-epoca-nid21012022/)).
- An earlier La Nación piece put it at about 3 million players in 1992, explicitly "without official statistics" ([La Nación](https://www.lanacion.com.ar/deportes/padel-un-deporte-que-intenta-resurgir-tras-sus-gloriosos-anos-noventas-nid2097305/)).
- Unidiversidad, the University of Cuyo's news site, cited about 3 million and called padel "the second most practised sport of the 90s" ([Unidiversidad](https://www.unidiversidad.com.ar/volvio-el-padel-tras-el-furor-de-los-90-el-deporte-renace-en-mendoza)).

The film should say "an estimated three to four million players, by unofficial counts".

The press agrees on why it collapsed [WS for the general account]:

- an oversupply of courts that "made padel lose its mystique"
- badly built courts whose "measurements were all different"
- injuries among players who started with no physical preparation
- the economic crisis that peaked in 2001, after which tournaments stopped paying and the best professionals emigrated to Spain

Sources: [mnews](https://mnews.com.ar/deportes/21678-El-padel-volvio-para-quedarse-se-construye-mas-de-una-cancha-por-dia-); [El Economista](https://eleconomista.com.ar/deportes/auge-padel-argentina-fenomeno-crecimiento-n91777); [DataClave](https://www.dataclave.com.ar/poder/el-padel--un-deporte-con-sello-menemista-que-regresa--que-politicos-juegan-_a6069d14e656b0833e6fbd4cb); [Unidiversidad](https://www.unidiversidad.com.ar/volvio-el-padel-tras-el-furor-de-los-90-el-deporte-renace-en-mendoza).

A revival was reported from 2016 ([Infobae, 2016](https://www.infobae.com/tendencias/2016/11/10/el-resurgimiento-del-padel-el-deporte-furor-de-los-90/)) and gathered pace during the pandemic ([Los Andes](https://www.losandes.com.ar/mas-deportes/padel-en-mendoza-fue-furor-en-los-90-y-resurgio-a-todo-ritmo-en-medio-de-la-pandemia)). The FIP's October 2026 report puts Argentina third in the world with 3,300 clubs, behind Spain and Italy ([Ámbito](https://www.ambito.com/negocios/el-padel-mueve-us7930-millones-el-mundo-y-argentina-es-tercera-cantidad-clubes-n6334087); [Sitio Andino](https://www.sitioandino.com.ar/andino-sports/el-padel-supera-los-40-millones-jugadores-y-argentina-esta-los-paises-mas-clubes-n5731948)) [WS]. Premier Padel's Buenos Aires event in May 2025 sold more than 50,000 tickets ([La Nación, 2025](https://www.lanacion.com.ar/deportes/padel/premier-padel-de-buenos-aires-mas-de-50000-entradas-vendidas-sold-out-y-un-impacto-exponencial-nid27052025/)) [SS].

Mexico itself took two decades to organise. Its "first official court", Ignacio Soto Borja's in Coyoacán, was opened by Corcuera in 1992 ([Beyond Line](https://beyondline.com.mx/numero-5/ignacio-soto-borja-y-anda-padel-deporte-mexicano/)) [SS], the year the Mexican federation was founded. By 2025 the FIP said Mexico had passed one million players ([FIP, Focus en México](https://www.padelfip.com/es/2025/03/focus-en-mexico-donde-el-padel-ha-superado-el-millon-de-practicantes/)) [SS].

## Spain became the heartland, then Europe, the Gulf and Britain caught the fever

Spain turned a pastime of the rich into a mass sport. Its national association dates from 1991 ([FIP (ES)](https://www.padelfip.com/es/historia/)). The FIP says Spain's national sports council, the Consejo Superior de Deportes (CSD), recognised padel as a sport in **1993** ([FIP, History](https://www.padelfip.com/history/)) [SS]. No date was found for when the Federación Española de Pádel itself became a federation.

Federation licences rose steadily:

- **6,137 in 2000** ([Padel Barcelona](https://padelbarcelona.es/padel/historico-y-comparacion-de-licencias-federativas-de-padel/)) [DW]
- **109,040 in 2024** ([FIP](https://www.padelfip.com/es/2025/02/padel-en-espana-crecimiento-de-pistas-y-licencias-de-jugadores-en-2024-morcillo-estos-resultados-reflejan-el-trabajo-duro-de-los-ultimos-anos/); [Infobae/EFE](https://www.infobae.com/espana/agencias/2025/05/30/el-padel-el-que-mas-crece-en-numero-de-federados-78-entre-los-deportes-mas-populares/)) [WS]
- **117,508 in 2025**, the fastest growth of any major federated sport that year ([Infobae/EFE, May 2026](https://www.infobae.com/espana/agencias/2026/05/29/el-padel-es-el-deporte-que-mas-crecio-en-fichas-en-2025-con-un-incremento-del-77/)) [SS, reporting the ministry yearbook]

An academic analysis of the CSD surveys estimated about **4.2 million** Spanish players in 2015 ([Courel-Ibáñez et al., 2017](https://ccd.ucam.edu/index.php/revista/article/view/830)) [SS].

The famous line that padel is "Spain's second sport" is true only with its qualifier. In 2015 padel ranked second among team or competitive sports, played by 9% of the population against football's 12% ([Padel Star, reporting the CSD](https://padelstar.es/noticias-padel/el-padel-es-el-segundo-deporte-mas-practicado/)) [DW]. The stronger fact comes straight from the **2022 CSD survey**: among Spaniards who did any sport, **15.8% played padel and 14.5% football** ([CSD, 2022](https://www.csd.gob.es/sites/default/files/media/files/2022-12/Encuesta%20de%20H%C3%A1bitos%20Deportivos%20en%20Espa%C3%B1a%202022.%20S%C3%ADntesis%20de%20resultados.pdf)) [WS, official primary]. A different survey, CIS study no. 3447 of March 2024, put padel at 9.3% of people who play sport ([CIS](https://www.cis.es/documents/d/guest/es3447mar-pdf)). The gap is a reminder that survey design moves these shares. The FIP counts about **17,300 Spanish courts in 2025**, the largest base in the world ([Statista](https://www.statista.com/statistics/1473603/padel-courts-worldwide/)) [SS], and **4,705 clubs** in 2026 ([Ámbito](https://www.ambito.com/negocios/el-padel-mueve-us7930-millones-el-mundo-y-argentina-es-tercera-cantidad-clubes-n6334087)) [SS].

Sweden is the sport's clearest boom and bust. The boom:

- **717 courts in 2019** ([Padelfeber](https://padelfeber.se/artiklar/2019/exakt-sa-stor-ar-nu-padeln-i-sverige/)) [SS]
- about **1,500 at the end of 2020** ([Swedish federation's application to the Swedish Sports Confederation](https://www.rf.se/download/18.407871d3183abb2a6131c1a/1665066578584/11.1%20Svenska%20Padelf%C3%B6rbundet.pdf)) [WS, official]
- about **3,500 in 2021**, when 540,000 people booked a court ([Svenska Padelförbundet](https://www.mynewsdesk.com/se/svenskpadel/pressreleases/540-000-svenskar-har-bokat-en-padelbana-2021-3147061)) [WS]

The bust:

- The number of people booking a court fell to **421,000 in 2022** ([Svenska Padelförbundet](https://svenskpadel.se/2023/03/31/svensk-padel-vaxer-sig-starkare/); [Padeldirekt](https://padeldirekt.se/2023/03/31/statsiken-visar-stor-minskning-100-000-farre-padelspelare-i-fjol)) [WS].
- Bloomberg reported that almost **90 padel-related companies** filed for bankruptcy in 2023, and that halls were being turned into warehouses and discount grocery stores ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/features/2023-09-16/sweden-holds-warning-for-potential-crash-of-the-the-4-billion-padel-craze)) [SS].
- Later counts say more than 100 facilities closed in 2022–24 ([European Business Magazine](https://europeanbusinessmagazine.com/sweden-padel-mania-bankruptcies/)) [DW].
- Oversupply and southern Sweden's electricity prices, about five times pre-2020 levels by winter 2022, are blamed only in weak sources ([studypadel](https://www.studypadel.com/articles/sweden-padel-boom-bust)) [DW].

The court total did not collapse: the FIP counts 4,220 Swedish courts in 2025 ([studypadel, FIP table](https://www.studypadel.com/articles/padel-courts-worldwide)) [DW]. The bust hit operators and how full the courts were, not the number of courts. The footballer **Zlatan Ibrahimović** became the face of Swedish padel. He co-founded the Padel Zenter chain, which opened a large complex in Sollentuna in November 2024 ([Padel Alto](https://padelalto.com/2024/11/15/zlatan-opens-mega-facility-in-sweden-i-believe-in-the-sport); [Tennis Tonic](https://tennistonic.com/tennis-news/802991/zlatan-ibrahimovic-opens-a-large-padel-facility-in-sweden-and-hopes-to-work-with-other-football-players/)) [WS].

Elsewhere in Europe:

- **Italy** is second in the world. It went from about **1,000 courts in 2019** to roughly **9,000 in 2023** ([The Padel Paper](https://thepadelpaper.com/italy-padel-industry-carlo-ferrara-fip/)) [SS] and **10,220 in 2025** by the FIP's count ([SuperTennis](https://www.supertennis.tv/News/Padel/fip-world-padel-2025-dati-italia-sviluppo-padel)) [SS]. Growth slowed to about 7–8% in 2024 ([NOENE](https://www.noene.it/blog/padel-i-numeri-in-italia/)) [SS]. The Italian tennis and padel federation counts 2.1 million padel players in 2025 ([FITP Report 2025](https://www.fitp.it/Federazione/News/Attivita-internazionale/report-tennis-padel-2025-fitp)) [SS, official].
- **Britain** is the current hotspot ([LTA, Jul 2025](https://www.ltapadel.org.uk/news/padels-rapid-rise-great-britain-reaches-1000-padel-courts/); [LTA, Mar 2026](https://www.ltapadel.org.uk/news/2026/march/860000-players-lta-unveils-latest-figures-on-growth-of-padel-in-britain/); [LTA, 1,000,000 players](https://ltapadel.org.uk/news/1000000-padel-players-in-britain)) [WS, official]. The LTA, the national tennis body, took over padel in 2019, when there were **68 courts** and 15,000 players. At the end of 2025 it counted **1,553 courts at 559 venues** and 860,000 people who had played at least once that year, and by **May 2026** it counted **1 million players**. The LTA's own releases disagree on its 2020 and end-2024 court counts.
- **The Netherlands** went from **475 courts in 2020** to **3,523 at 780 locations at the end of 2025**, with 876,000 players ([KNLTB/EY](https://www.knltb.nl/media/xvtejmk4/eyxknltb_padel_in_cijfers-2025.pdf); [KNLTB](https://www.knltb.nl/nieuws/2026/03/knltb-en-ey-presenteren-tweede-editie-padel-in-cijfers/)) [WS, official].
- **France**: the national tennis federation (FFT) counted **2,917 courts** in 2025, up 40%, and passed **100,000 padel licences** in June 2025 ([FFT](https://www.fft.fr/actualites/padel-expansion-chiffres-2025-pistes-pratiquants-licencies); [FFT](https://www.fft.fr/actualites/la-barre-des-100-000-licencies-padel-atteinte-2025)) [WS].
- **Belgium, Denmark and Portugal**: Belgium (2,150 courts), Denmark (1,575) and Portugal (1,560) also sit in the FIP's 2025 top ten ([sgbonline](https://sgbonline.com/exec-study-finds-padel-boasting-robust-growth/); [studypadel](https://www.studypadel.com/articles/padel-in-scandinavia-2025)) [DW, secondary tallies].

In the Gulf, padel's weight lies in money and events more than in courts:

- **UAE**: its first courts date to about 2013, according to one interview ([AGSI](https://agsi.org/analysis/padel-picks-up-the-worlds-fastest-growing-sport-finds-a-home-in-the-gulf/)) [SS].
- **Qatar**: a family of Qatari brothers built the first two outdoor courts in Doha in summer 2017 ([Kuwait Times](https://kuwaittimes.com/the-other-world-cup-qatari-brothers-inspire-padel-boom/)), or in July 2018 by another account ([The Padel School](https://thepadelschool.com/resources/padel-tips/padel-start-in-qatar)) [DW].
- **Kuwait**: the FIP says its roughly 580 courts give the best ratio in Asia, about one court per 7,400 people ([FIP, Focus on Kuwait](https://www.padelfip.com/2024/11/focus-on-kuwait-the-p1-premier-after-the-fip-world-padel-championships-qualifiers/)) [SS, official].
- **The region**: the Middle East holds 72% of Asia's padel facilities ([Padel Addict, FIP 2025](https://www.padeladdict.com/fip-world-padel-report-2025-cifras-globales-del-padel/)) [SS].

**The United States** is small but growing fast. Individual membership of its federation, the USPA, rose 53.5% to **2,915** in 2025, across 108 clubs ([USPA 2025](https://padelusa.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/2025-ANNUAL-REPORT-JAN-22.pdf)) [WS, official]. The country's thousandth court opened in Miami around April 2026 ([actu-padel](https://actu-padel.com/en/1000-padel-courts-in-the-usa-the-boom-changes-scale/)) [SS].

The FIP's 2024 report put about 70% of the world's courts in Europe ([FIP 2024](https://www.padelfip.com/2024/05/world-padel-report-2024-the-first-official-fip-report-on-the-padel-movement/)) [WS, official]. The rest were spread as follows:

- South America: 14,850
- Asia: 3,200
- Africa: 2,300
- Oceania: fewer than 100

By 2025 Egypt (about 1,500 courts) and South Africa (1,210) led Africa ([SGI Europe](https://www.sgieurope.com/consumer/padel-is-in-constant-growth-around-the-world-says-latest-fip-report/118582.article)) [SS].

The pandemic sped up a boom that had already begun rather than starting one. Each of these figures is [WS]:

- Spanish licences jumped 28% from 2020 to 2021 ([Cluster Pádel](https://www.clusterpadel.com/noticias-padel/20231107/federacion-espanola-padel-sobrepasa-barrera-historica-100000-licencias.aspx)).
- Swedish courts more than doubled in 2021 ([Svenska Padelförbundet](https://www.mynewsdesk.com/se/svenskpadel/pressreleases/540-000-svenskar-har-bokat-en-padelbana-2021-3147061)).
- British courts rose from 87 to 150 ([LTA](https://www.ltapadel.org.uk/news/padels-rapid-rise-great-britain-reaches-1000-padel-courts/)).
- 2022 was the best year in the Playtomic series, with 28% more courts ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.co.uk/news-releases/playtomic-and-monitor-deloitte-report-the-padel-industry-will-grow-to-reach-6-billion-euros-in-2026-301861180.html)).

No study isolates a pandemic effect, though, and growth in Spain and Sweden began before 2020 ([Padel Magazine](https://padel-magazine.co.uk/the-boom-of-padel-during-the-post-covid-period/)) [DW for cause]. Narrate "the pandemic accelerated it".

## Three founding associations became 115 federations and one tour backed by Qatar

Organisation followed the Argentine boom. A group of friends led by **Oscar "Cacho" Nicastro** founded the **Asociación Argentina de Pádel** (APA), with **Alberto Villaverde** as its first president. The FIP calls it the world's first national association and dates it to **1988** ([FIP (ES)](https://www.padelfip.com/es/historia/); [Red Bull](https://redbull.com/es-es/historia-del-padel)) [WS]. Tiempo Argentino instead names Mauricio Macri among its founding members in **1987** ([Tiempo Argentino](https://www.tiempoar.com.ar/nota/redencion-presidencial-para-un-deporte-que-fue-boom-en-los-noventa)) [DW on the year]. Spain's association followed in 1991.

On **12 July 1991** the Argentine, Spanish and Uruguayan bodies founded the **International Padel Federation** (FIP) at the Club de Golf La Moraleja in Madrid. **Julio Alegría Artiach** of Bilbao was its first president ([FIP, History](https://www.padelfip.com/history/); [FIP By-Laws 2021](https://www.padelfip.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/1-ByLaws_FIP_Eng_2021.pdf)) [WS, official]. The same meeting set the rules for the first World Championship. Mexico's federation, Femepa, followed on **27 April 1992** ([FIP, Focus en México](https://www.padelfip.com/es/2025/03/focus-en-mexico-donde-el-padel-ha-superado-el-millon-de-practicantes/)) [SS, official]. No source found names the FIP presidents between 1991 and 2018. The Italian **Luigi Carraro** was elected in November 2018, during a chaotic World Championship in Paraguay ([Ubitennis](https://ubitennis.com/blog/2018/11/08/mondiali-di-padel-un-flop-inatteso-carraro-eletto-presidente-della-fip)). He was re-elected in 2021 and again, unanimously, in Doha in October 2024 ([FIP](https://www.padelfip.com/es/2024/10/luigi-carraro-reelegido-por-unanimidad-presidente-de-la-fip-gracias-por-la-confianza-nos-esperan-grandes-desafios-y-los-ganaremos-juntos/); [Sky Sport Italia](https://sport.sky.it/altri-sport/2024/10/28/padel-carraro-presidente-fip)) [WS].

Membership crept up for almost three decades, then surged:

- A weak timeline counts 11 federations by the end of the 1990s ([Tennis Giant](https://tennisgiant.com/blogs/all-about-padel/key-milestones-in-the-history-of-padel)) [DW].
- Carraro has spoken of 32 in 2019 ([Sky Sport Italia](https://sport.sky.it/altri-sport/2024/10/28/padel-carraro-presidente-fip)) [SS].
- The European Olympic Committees counted 51 in May 2021 ([EOC](https://www.eurolympic.org/padel-to-make-multi-sport-games-debut-at-krakow-malopolska-2023/)) [SS].
- The FIP's own counts are [WS, official]:
  - **71** in July 2023 ([FIP](https://www.padelfip.com/2023/07/fip-general-assembly-from-new-members-to-equal-prize-money-to-the-new-senior-circuit/))
  - **81** in May 2024 ([FIP](https://www.padelfip.com/2024/05/in-asuncion-paraguay-the-fip-general-assembly-welcomes-10-new-member-countries-carraro-united-and-cohesive-we-have-won-crucial-challenges-and-will-build-our-future-this-way/))
  - **100** at the Acapulco General Assembly in November 2025 ([FIP](https://www.padelfip.com/2025/11/35th-fip-general-assembly-concludes-in-acapulco-14-new-national-federations-join-as-fip-reaches-100-members-confirming-padels-global-momentu/))
  - **115** in Madrid on **12 October 2026** ([FIP](https://www.padelfip.com/2026/10/fip-general-assembly-strengthens-global-unity-as-membership-reaches-115-federations/))

The sport also worked its way into multi-sport games:

- **2022 South American Games**: the FIP lists them as an earlier step ([FIP](https://www.padelfip.com/2025/11/milestone-moment-in-sport-as-padel-set-for-asian-games-debut-international-padel-federation-officially-recognised-by-olympic-council-of-asia/)) [SS].
- **2023 European Games, Kraków** (21–25 June): the FIP called them "padel's major multi-sport debut". Spain's **Daniel Santigosa** was the only double gold medallist ([FIP](https://www.padelfip.com/2023/06/the-stage-for-padels-major-multi-sport-debut/); [Padel Alto](https://padelalto.com/2023/06/26/surprise-in-the-finals-of-the-2023-european-games-here-are-all-the-medals)) [WS].
- **FISU**, the world university sports federation, recognised padel on **6 February 2025** ([FISU](https://www.fisu.net/2025/02/06/fisu-grants-official-recognition-to-the-international-padel-federation/)) [WS].
- The **Olympic Council of Asia** recognised the FIP in **November 2025** ([FIP](https://www.padelfip.com/2025/11/milestone-moment-in-sport-as-padel-set-for-asian-games-debut-international-padel-federation-officially-recognised-by-olympic-council-of-asia/)) [WS].
- **Mediterranean Games, Taranto**, 22–28 August 2026: a medal debut ([FIP](https://www.padelfip.com/2026/07/padel-set-to-make-its-debut-as-a-medal-sport-at-the-mediterranean-games-taranto-2026/)) [WS].
- **Asian Games, Aichi-Nagoya**, 28 September–3 October 2026: a medal debut ([FIP](https://www.padelfip.com/2026/03/padel-confirmed-as-medal-sport-at-the-aichi-nagoya-2026-asian-games/)) [WS]. Wikipedia lists Qatar as the men's gold medallists ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Padel_at_the_2026_Asian_Games)) [DW].

Padel is **not an Olympic sport**. It was not at Paris 2024 and will not be at Los Angeles 2028 ([padel-rules.com](https://padel-rules.com/history-and-culture/padel-and-olympics/)) [SS]. The programme for Brisbane 2032 is due in 2027 ([The Padel Paper](https://thepadelpaper.com/padel-olympic-games-fip-luigi-carraro/)). Blogs disagree on whether the International Olympic Committee (IOC) recognises the FIP at all, and no IOC source was found ([padel-rules.com](https://padel-rules.com/history-and-culture/padel-and-olympics/); [justpadel](https://justpadel.com/en-in/blogs/blog/will-padel-become-an-olympic-sport)) [DW]. The film should say only that the Olympics are the FIP's stated goal.

The **first World Championship** was played in **September 1992** in Madrid and Seville, with the finals at **La Cartuja during Expo 92**. Argentina won both the men's and women's titles, Spain took silver and Uruguay bronze ([FIP, Roll of Honour](https://www.padelfip.com/roll-of-honour-world/); [El Gráfico](https://elgrafico.com.ar/articulo/maradona-y-el-grafico/54637/30-anios-maradona-su-llegada-a-sevilla-y-el-primer-mundial-de-padel)) [WS]. The team event is held roughly every two years. By the available tallies, every title decided so far has gone to Argentina or Spain:

- **Argentina's men** have **12 titles**, the latest in 2024 ([Infobae/EFE](https://www.infobae.com/america/agencias/2024/11/02/argentina-gana-a-espana-en-la-final-masculina-y-logra-su-duodecimo-titulo-mundial-de-padel/); [La Nación](https://www.lanacion.com.ar/deportes/canchallena/asi-quedo-la-tabla-de-campeones-del-mundial-de-padel-tras-la-consagracion-de-la-argentina-nid05112022/)) [WS].
- **Spain's men** have **four**: 1998, 2008, 2010 and 2021 ([Wikipedia (es)](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Campeonato_Mundial_de_P%C3%A1del)) [DW; verify on the FIP roll of honour].
- **Asunción 2018**: the men's final was never played. After rain and venue changes, Spain and Argentina refused the venue on safety grounds ([Padel Addict](https://www.padeladdict.com/razones-desastre-mundial-padel-paraguay/); [Ubitennis](https://ubitennis.com/blog/2018/11/08/mondiali-di-padel-un-flop-inatteso-carraro-eletto-presidente-della-fip)) [WS].
- **Spain's women** have won six in a row (2014–2024) and **nine** in all, against Argentina's **eight** ([Diari de Tarragona](https://www.diaridetarragona.com/deportes/199924/ari-sanchez-campeona-del-mundo-de-padel-con-espana-fg21723240.html); [El Gráfico](https://www.elgrafico.com.ar/articulo/padel/58737/mundial-de-padel-argentina-se-clasifico-con-puntaje-perfecto)) [WS for Spain; SS for Argentina's eight].
- **Doha 2024**: in the November final Argentina beat Spain 2–1 for its 12th men's title, and Spain's women won their ninth ([Qatar Tribune](https://qatar-tribune.com/article/147670/sports/argentina-spain-celebrate-world-padel-title-triumphs-in-doha/amp)) [WS].
- **2026**: renamed the **FIP World Cup**, the event is scheduled for Doha on 2–7 November 2026. Its prize money is a record **€1.2 million**, split equally between men and women ([Padel Addict](https://www.padeladdict.com/doha-fip-world-cup-mundial-padel-2026-2028-sede-premios-record/); [Bloomberg Línea](https://www.bloomberglinea.com/linea-deportiva/estos-seran-los-premios-del-mundial-de-padel-2026/)) [WS]. It had **not been played** on 16 October 2026.

Professional padel has had four eras:

1. **A Beefeater circuit** preceded everything else ([Wikipedia, Padel Pro Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Padel_Pro_Tour)) [DW].
2. **The Padel Pro Tour** was founded in 2005 by the men's and women's players' associations (the AJPP and AFEP) and promoters, and ran seven seasons, 2006–2012 ([Wikipedia, Padel Pro Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Padel_Pro_Tour)) [DW].
3. **The World Padel Tour** took over in **2013** after the players' association backed it. It was run by **Setpoint Events, a subsidiary of the brewer Damm**, and lasted eleven editions to 2023 ([padel1969](https://padel1969.com/world-tour/); [Padel Addict](https://www.padeladdict.com/en/premier-padel-and-world-padel-tour-will-be-unified-in-2024/)) [WS].
4. **Premier Padel** was launched by the FIP in **March 2022**, backed by Qatar Sports Investments (QSI), the fund chaired by Paris Saint-Germain's president **Nasser Al-Khelaifi**, and supported by the players' association PPA ([El Español](https://elespanol.com/deportes/otros-deportes/20220315/nace-premier-padel-impulsado-fip-nasser-al-khelaifi/657434441_0.html)) [WS].

Premier Padel's first event was the **Qatar Major** in Doha, 28 March–2 April 2022 ([Qatar Tribune](https://www.qatar-tribune.com/article/231150/SPORTS/Top-players-gear-up-for-Ooredoo-Qatar-Major-2022-%E2%80%93-first-event-of-newly-launched-global-padel-tour/amp)) [WS]. That year it added Majors at Rome's Foro Italico and in Monterrey, and one at Roland-Garros on Court Philippe-Chatrier ([Tennis.com](https://www.tennis.com/news/articles/padel-comes-to-roland-garros-court-philippe-chatrier-the-latest-non-tennis-event)) [WS]. In 2022 a Premier Padel Major paid **€525,000**, against **€110,880** for WPT's top Masters Final ([Padel Alto](https://padelalto.com/2022/04/05/the-list-how-the-prize-money-differs-in-premier-padel-wpt-and-apt)) [SS].

Two years of conflict followed: a complaint to the European Commission, and WPT lawsuits against players, the FIP, the PPA and QSI ([Sportico](https://www.sportico.com/leagues/other-sports/2023/qatar-sports-investments-buys-world-padel-tour-1234735097/)) [WS]. It ended on **24 August 2023**, when QSI agreed to buy WPT from Damm for an undisclosed price and the two tours were merged under FIP governance from 2024 ([FIP](https://www.padelfip.com/2023/08/qatar-sports-investments-qsi-and-damm-reach-historic-agreement-for-acquisition-of-world-padel-tour-wpt-by-qsi-with-premier-padel-and-wpt-to-be-unified-under-governance-of-international-padel-fede/); [SportsPro](https://www.sportspro.com/news/premier-padel-world-padel-tour-qsi-damm-merger-fip/)) [WS].

The 2026 "Qatar Airways Premier Padel Tour" has **26 events in 18 countries**, including four Majors (Doha, Rome, Paris and Acapulco). Its Finals are in Barcelona on 7–13 December ([FIP](https://www.padelfip.com/2025/12/premier-padel-and-international-padel-federation-unveil-2026-qatar-airways-premier-padel-tour-calendar-and-innovative-new-star-point-system-launched); [Padel Addict](https://www.padeladdict.com/en/premier-padel-confirms-its-2026-calendar-with-26-tournaments-in-18-countries/)) [WS]. Around it:

- The FIP's own **CUPRA FIP Tour** feeds into it, with more than 200 events in 2026 ([FIP](https://www.padelfip.com/2026/02/cupra-fip-tour-2026-more-than-200-combined-tournaments-and-17-new-countries-for-an-increasingly-global-padel/)) [SS, official].
- **A1 Padel** (formerly the APT Padel Tour, founded 2020) ran a rival third circuit. It was renamed in January 2023 after Yankee Global Enterprises, owner of the New York Yankees, took a stake ([Padel Addict](https://www.padeladdict.com/apt-padel-tour-cambia-de-nombre-a-a1-padel-y-anuncia-su-calendario-para-este-2023/); [Padel Alto](https://padelalto.com/2023/01/24/fabrice-pastor-on-a1-padel-we-are-in-the-sights-of-international-companies)) [WS]. Its status in 2024–26 is unknown.
- **Team padel**, born in the Hexagon Cup, moves to the FIP-sanctioned **Hexagon World Series** from January 2027 ([FIP](https://www.padelfip.com/2026/08/hexagon-world-series-and-international-padel-federation-announce-generali-hexagon-cup-madrid-as-its-inaugural-event-and-2027-season-opener/); [Sportcal](https://www.sportcal.com/news/generali-to-title-sponsor-first-hexagon-world-series-event-in-madrid/)) [WS].

The 2026 season's total prize money ("over $15 million") rests only on blogs ([PadelWorld NZ](https://padelworld.co.nz/articles/padel-prize-money)) [DW] and should not be narrated.

## Argentines ruled the rankings for decades; Spanish–Argentine pairs rule in 2026

The men's game has had few dynasties. Argentina's **Alejandro Lasaigues** was number one for four years to 1991 with Horacio Álvarez Clementi. He then held it for five years "without losing a single match" with **Roby Gattiker**, and won the team World Championship in 1992, 1994 and 1996 ([Unidiversidad](https://www.unidiversidad.com.ar/alejandro-lasaigues-es-el-nuevo-director-del-mendoza-premier-padel); [Mendoza government](https://www.mendoza.gov.ar/prensa/el-mendoza-premier-padel-tendra-a-una-leyenda-como-director/)) [SS, regional press].

## The 20 × 10 m court survived while glass, carbon and the Star Point arrived

The founding layout survives almost unchanged. The FIP's description of the 1969 court (20 × 10 m, 3 m walls, a tennis net) matches the 2026 *Rules of Padel* ([FIP, History](https://www.padelfip.com/history/); [FIP Rules of Padel 2026](https://www.padelfip.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/FIP_Rules-of-Padel.pdf)) [WS, official]. The 2026 rules specify:

- a 10 × 20 m interior, within ±0.5%
- a net 0.88 m high at the centre and 0.92 m at the posts
- end walls 4 m high: a 3 m wall topped by 1 m of metal mesh
- a wall of "any transparent or solid material (crystal, bricks, etc.)", so brick is still permitted
- at least 6 m of clear height above the court, with 8 m suggested for new facilities

What changed is the material and the look, and here the sources are mostly court builders and blogs:

- **Walls**: the first courts were cement slabs without turf or glass ([GIMPADEL](https://gimpadel.com/2018/10/26/evolucion-pistas-padel/)) [DW]. Courts mixing wall and glass appeared in South America in the late 1980s and early 1990s, and glass spread through the 1990s ([Ferrobalpadel](http://ferrobalpadel.com/pistas-de-padel-de-muros-vs-pista-de-padel-de-cristal/)) [DW]. Another blog puts the move to glass in 2000–2010 ([padelagogo](https://www.padelagogo.com/la-evolucion-de-las-pistas-de-padel/)) [DW].
- **The first glass court**: a vendor credits it to Mar del Plata in **1989**: the transportable "Crystal Palace", designed by **Jorge Galeotti** ([Amarantoidea](https://www.amarantoidea.com/en/come-nasce-il-padel/)) [DW, a single vendor].
- **Turf**: artificial turf replaced bare cement, with nylon fibres giving way to polypropylene. No source gives a date ([padelazo](https://www.padelazo.com/2020/05/cesped-artificial-para-pistas-de-padel/)) [DW].
- **The panoramic court**: this design, with no pillars in the glass end walls, was made for television. Its manufacturer, Padel10, says the tour commissioned it at the end of 2012 and that it debuted at the World Padel Tour's first event, in Murcia in 2013 ([Padel10](https://padel10.com/la-primera-pista-de-padel-world-padel-tour.html)) [SS, the manufacturer's own claim].
- **Colour**: courts of the 1990s and early 2000s were almost all green. The WPT made blue the television standard because it contrasts with the yellow ball ([actu-padel](https://actu-padel.com/en/padel-court-why-has-blue-become-the-standard-color-in-padel/)) [DW]. A brief season of black turf in 2015 is told two ways ([Mondo](https://www.mondoworldwide.com/emea/en/spaziomondo/projects/project-world-padel-tour/)) [DW].

The racket's history before the rulebook comes only from shops and blogs [DW throughout]:

- **Wood came first**: paddles of about 450 g and about 10 mm thick ([padelspain.net](https://www.padelspain.net/material/10549/Evolucion-de-las-palas-de-padel-de-la-madera-al-carbono/)).
- **A rubber (EVA) core** arrived in Argentina, credited either to the brand SANE ([newpadel](https://www.newpadel.net/blog/evolucion-palas-de-padel/)) or to Carlos Pérez of Buenos Aires in 1989 ([padelandia](https://www.padelandia.com/padel/la-paleta-de-goma/)). It brought weight down to about 390 g by the late 1990s.
- **Fibreglass and carbon faces** over foam or EVA cores followed from the 2000s ([padelspain.net](https://www.padelspain.net/material/10549/Evolucion-de-las-palas-de-padel-de-la-madera-al-carbono/)).

Today's limits come from the FIP rules [WS, official]:

- total length at most **45.5 cm**
- width at most **26 cm**
- thickness at most **38 mm**
- cylindrical holes of 9–13 mm in the centre
- a non-elastic wrist cord of at most 35 cm, which must be worn
- **no weight limit**

The ball is close to a tennis ball:

| | Weight | Diameter | Bounce from a 2.54 m drop |
|---|---|---|---|
| Padel (FIP) | 56.0–59.4 g | 6.35–6.77 cm | 135–145 cm |
| Tennis (ITF) | 56.0–59.4 g | not verified | about 135–147 cm |

Sources: [FIP Rules](https://www.padelfip.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/FIP-Rules-of-Padel.pdf); [ITF](https://m.itftennis.com/media/2100/balls-ball-approval-tests.pdf); [ITF Rules of Tennis 2018](https://hts.hr/wp-content/uploads/__2019/07/2018_ITF_Rules_of_Tennis.pdf) [WS].

The FIP's pressure figure, "between 4.6 kg and 5.2 kg per 2.54 square cm", reads like a garbled "per square inch" ([FIP Rules](https://www.padelfip.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/FIP-Rules-of-Padel.pdf)) [DW on the number]. Quote it exactly or say "lower pressure than a tennis ball". FIP-approved ball lists exist from 2020 ([FIP](https://www.padelfip.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/FIP-Approved-Padel-Balls-2020.pdf)). When balls made specifically for padel first appeared is unknown.

The core rules have not changed since the beginning ([FIP Rules 2026](https://www.padelfip.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/FIP_Rules-of-Padel.pdf); [LTA Padel rules](https://www.ltapadel.org.uk/play/how-to-get-started-playing-padel/rules/)) [WS]:

- The serve is underhand: the ball is bounced and struck at or below the waist into the diagonal box.
- Players may play the ball off the walls on their own side.
- A shot that hits the opponents' walls or fence before bouncing loses the point.
- Scoring is tennis scoring.

Running out of the court to play the ball back is a codified rule (Rule 16 in the 2021 and 2026 revisions). It applies only where the organisers authorise it in advance and the court has the required safety zone outside it ([FIP Reglamento](https://www.padelfip.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/2-Reglamento-Juego.pdf); [FIP Reglas 2026](https://www.padelfip.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/FIP_Reglas-del-Padel.pdf)) [WS]. When it was first allowed was not found.

The modern changes are at deuce:

- **2020, the golden point.** The World Padel Tour made it mandatory from the 2020 season, with its debut at the Marbella Master: at 40–40 a single point decides the game ([Padel Addict](https://www.padeladdict.com/punto-de-oro-world-padel-tour/)). Two academic studies found more games per set and more breaks of serve afterwards ([UNEX](https://dehesa.unex.es/handle/10662/17269); [UCAM](https://ccd.ucam.edu/visores/18_56/1966-en/index.html)) [WS].
- **March 2022, back to advantage.** Premier Padel played advantage from its first event ([Padel Alto](https://padelalto.com/2022/03/30/day-2-from-doha-finally-the-golden-point-is-gone-and-all-three-relatives-involved-in-todays-surprises)) [SS].
- **28 November 2025, the Star Point.** At the General Assembly in Acapulco, the FIP's 100 federations unanimously approved it. At deuce, advantage is played twice; at a third deuce one deciding "Star Point" is played, with the receiving pair choosing their side ([FIP](https://www.padelfip.com/2025/12/between-innovation-and-tradition-introducing-the-star-point-the-scoring-system-that-appeals-to-everyone/); [FIP Rules 2026](https://www.padelfip.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/FIP_Rules-of-Padel.pdf)) [WS, official].
- **Into play.** The Star Point was announced on 18 December 2025 and came into force on 1 January 2026. It was first used on the CUPRA FIP Tour in Melbourne in January, and on Premier Padel at the Riyadh P1 on 9–14 February 2026 ([Premier Padel](https://premierpadel.com/en/news/premier-padel-and-international-padel-federation-unveil-2026-qatar-airways-premier-padel-tour-calendar-and-innovative-new-star-point-system-launched)) [WS].

The 2026 rules keep all three options (advantage, Star Point and golden point) for other organisers. They also relax the serve stance: the server now needs one foot behind the line, where the 2023 text required both ([FIP Rules 2026](https://www.padelfip.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/FIP_Rules-of-Padel.pdf); [FIP 2023](https://www.padelfip.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/2-game-regulations.pdf)) [WS]. Blogs report other 2026 changes: an instant lost point for a broken wrist cord, shorter warm-ups, and new ball colours. None was confirmed on the FIP's own site ([padelbarcelona](https://padelbarcelona.es/padel/nuevas-normas-padel-2026-reglamento-fip-guia-completa/)) [DW].

## Padel has 58,000 to 91,000 courts and 19 to 40 million players, depending on who counts

Two report families measure padel, and they differ by design:

| | Playtomic Global Padel Report | FIP World Padel Report |
|---|---|---|
| Method | Counts bookable clubs, from the ground up | Surveys national federations and counts every playable court, including hotels and residential complexes ([Padel Business Magazine](https://newsletter.padelbusinessmagazine.com/p/fip-report-global-padel-courts-exceed-77-000-up-15-2-in-2025)) |
| Latest edition | 2026 edition, counting to the end of 2025 | 2026 edition, released around 14 October 2026 |
| Courts | **58,334**, about six times the 2016 base | **91,234**, "a new court every 36 minutes" |
| Clubs | about 20,900 | **28,497** |
| Players | **19.4 million** | **more than 40 million**, of whom 1.11 million are registered with federations |
| Also reports | the **first signs of overcapacity** | **191 countries and territories**; an **€8.6 billion** contribution to world GDP |
| Sources | [Playtomic](https://playtomic.com/global-padel-report); [Padel Addict](https://www.padeladdict.com/en/padel-is-experiencing-unstoppable-growth-more-than-58000-courts-worldwide-and-19-4-million-players/); [actu-padel](https://actu-padel.com/en/global-padel-report-2026-world-padel-faces-the-challenge-of-overcapacity/) | [FIP, Oct 2026](https://www.padelfip.com/2026/10/fip-world-padel-report-2026-is-online-more-than-40-million-players-courts-up-by-20-and-a-new-study-on-economic-impact-e8-6-billion-in-gdp/); [Infobae/EFE](https://www.infobae.com/america/agencias/2026/10/14/el-padel-suma-mas-de-40-millones-de-jugadores-y-un-impacto-economico-de-8600-millones-en-el-mundo/) |
| Grade | [WS] | [WS that the FIP reported these; the player figure is an estimate, not a count] |

Playtomic worked with Monitor Deloitte in 2022–23 and has worked with PwC's Strategy& since 2024. The FIP's headline growth claim of +20.4% does not follow from its previous edition's 77,300 courts. The difference between 77,300 and 91,234 is +18%, so the totals should be treated as specific to each edition and the percentages as the FIP's own.

Neither family takes a census. Adding up the national player figures cited above for seven countries gives roughly 11–13 million:

| Country | Players |
|---|---|
| Spain | about 4.2 million (2015 estimate) |
| Argentina | 2–3 million (unofficial) |
| Italy | about 2 million |
| United States | about 1 million (SFIA, played at least once in 2025) |
| Netherlands | 0.88 million |
| Britain | under 1 million |
| Sweden | 0.4–0.7 million |

That makes Playtomic's 19.4 million plausible for regular players. The FIP's 40 million is plausible only with a loose definition such as "played at least once" (this is the research team's sanity check, not a published figure). The US entry shows how far definitions stretch. The USPA has 2,915 members, while an SFIA-based estimate says about 1.07 million Americans played in 2025 ([PadelFormat](https://padelformat.com/articles/us-padel-statistics)) [DW].

The honest lines for the film are:

- "between about 20 and 40 million players, depending on who counts and how"
- "between about 58,000 club courts and 91,000 courts of every kind"

Both families agree on the pace: roughly 15–20% more courts a year in 2022–2025.

Forecasts have repeatedly run ahead of reality [WS]:

- The 2023 Playtomic/Deloitte report predicted **85,000 courts by 2026** and a €6 billion industry ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.co.uk/news-releases/playtomic-and-monitor-deloitte-report-the-padel-industry-will-grow-to-reach-6-billion-euros-in-2026-301861180.html)).
- The 2024 edition cut that to about 70,000 by 2026 ([The Padel Paper](https://thepadelpaper.com/playtomic-global-padel-report-2024/)).
- The 2026 edition pushes about 91,000 back to **2028** ([Playtomic](https://playtomic.com/global-padel-report)).

With a year to go, Playtomic counted 58,334 courts at the end of 2025 on its own method. That is about two-thirds of the 85,000 it had forecast for 2026. The FIP's 91,234 matches that old forecast only because it counts differently. The two series should never share a chart axis without a label.

The 2026 Playtomic report sorts markets into types ([padel1969](https://padel1969.com/global-padel-report-2026/)) [DW, a secondary summary]:

- "heartlands": Spain, Italy, Portugal and Argentina
- "hotspots": the United Kingdom and Germany
- "post-boom adjustment": Sweden, Finland and Chile

The same secondary summary says Spain's growth has slowed to about 1.9%.

## Myths not to repeat

- **"Padel began on 19th-century British cruise ships."** No primary evidence exists. The documented deck game is deck tennis ([Platform Tennis Hall of Fame](https://platformtennishalloffame.org/history-of-the-game/)).
- **"Corcuera converted his squash court."** Only English Wikipedia says so. Every other account describes a frontón, a garden, or the space for a tennis court he could not fit ([Wikipedia (es)](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/P%C3%A1del); [Minter Dial](https://www.minterdial.com/2024/02/enrique-corcuera/)).
- **"Platform tennis was played in New York and Washington in the 1910s."** That was paddle tennis, in Washington Square Park in 1915. Platform tennis dates from 1928 ([Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/sports/platform-tennis)).
- **"Paddle tennis was invented in New York in 1921."** Beal devised it in Albion, Michigan, and moved to New York later ([Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Frank-P-Beal)).
- **"Spain's first court was built in 1970."** It was 1974 ([Marbella Club](https://www.marbellaclub.com/blog/marbella-club-anniversary-trivia)).
- **"The original Acapulco or Marbella court still stands."** Marbella's went in 1995. Corcuera's was demolished for a pool after his death in 1999 ([Marbella Club](https://www.marbellaclub.com/blog/marbella-club-anniversary-trivia); [Padel Magazine](https://padel-magazine.co.uk/viviana-corcuera-hubby-is-the-inventor-of-padel/)).
- **"Viviana was Hohenlohe's wife."** She was Corcuera's. Hohenlohe married Ira von Fürstenberg ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ira_von_F%C3%BCrstenberg)).
- **"Hohenlohe changed the court's dimensions and rules."** No source names any change.
- **"Manolo Santana ran the first padel tournaments in the 1970s."** No reputable source says so.
- **"PADEL is an acronym."** P.A.D.E.L. was a device to register the Spanish association. The word comes from "paddle tennis" ([DLE](https://dle.rae.es/p%C3%A1del)).
- **"Padel is Spain's second most played sport,"** said without its qualifier. In 2022, among Spaniards who play any sport, padel edged out football, 15.8% to 14.5% ([CSD](https://www.csd.gob.es/sites/default/files/media/files/2022-12/Encuesta%20de%20H%C3%A1bitos%20Deportivos%20en%20Espa%C3%B1a%202022.%20S%C3%ADntesis%20de%20resultados.pdf)).
- **"Argentina had 4.5 million players and 35,000 courts in 1993."** No locatable source exists.
- **"Padel is an Olympic sport", "the IOC recognises it", or "it will be Olympic by 2032".** None of these is supported.
- **"Kraków 2023 was padel's first multi-sport games."** The FIP itself lists the 2022 South American Games first.
- **"Padel was played at the Bernabéu."** The only racket event there in 2026 was a clay tennis practice court ([Real Madrid](https://www.realmadrid.com/en-US/news/club/latest-news/el-tenis-llega-al-bernabeu-23-04-2026)).
- **"Covid caused the boom."** It accelerated one already under way.
- **"Padel has 40 million players,"** stated flat. Say "the FIP estimates".

## Conclusion

The uncertainty in padel's origin story comes from the way it was told, not from gaps that further research will easily close. The earliest primary evidence anywhere in this history is a 1951 newsreel of a different game. Everything about Acapulco reached print decades later, through people with a stake in it: a widow, a son, a hotel marketing its heritage, and a federation building a founding myth while it seeks Olympic status. That is why the dates harden as they are repeated. The firmest "firsts" are institutional: 12 July 1991 in Madrid, and the 1992 World Championship at Expo 92. The romantic firsts (1969, 1974, the first glass court, the first panoramic court) each rest on one interested teller. For a channel that states only what it can source, this is the story rather than a problem with it: three dates, two brothers, two people credited with the rules, and two global counts.

The numbers show one pattern again and again: a boom, then oversupply, then a correction. Argentina built, by unofficial counts, tens of thousands of courts and watched clubs abandon them after 2001. Sweden roughly quintupled its courts in two years and lost about 90 companies in 2023. Playtomic's 2023 forecast of 85,000 courts by 2026 has been pushed back edition by edition to about 91,000 by 2028, and its 2026 report now warns of overcapacity. Through all of it the 20 × 10 m court has not moved, while power has. The best players came from Argentina, mass participation from Spain, and the money from Qatar, which staged the first Premier Padel event and hosts the 2024, 2026 and 2028 world team events. In October 2026 both world number-one pairs join a Spaniard and an Argentine. The tour's last regular-season Major of 2026 is in Acapulco, where the FIP approved the Star Point in 2025. The game the Corcueras played in their garden returns there as part of a tour financed from Qatar.
