# Running order and narration: Anthropic's IPO (film 31, full film)

The narration is word for word: lines L01 to L72. **Every line is spoken, in order, once: none
skipped, none merged away, none reworded.** Each line says what is on screen while it is spoken.
The film explains every idea a newcomer would need, with a simple picture for it; it never assumes
the viewer knows finance or AI terms.

Pictures: upload1 Yahoo Finance page, upload2 TNW page, upload3 Polymarket's post, upload4 the
market-value ranking (one card), upload5 Amazon's 10-Q passage (one card), upload6 the build board
(The Register top, Gallup bottom), upload7 the price board (Anthropic's price table left, Anthropic
quoted by Simon Willison top right, SemiAnalysis's chart bottom right), upload8 the risks board
(Fortune top, ABC News bottom), upload9 the social board (Theo top left, Gary Marcus top right, Brad
Gerstner bottom left, Hacker News bottom right). Charts C1-C17 are in doc2.

A one-card picture (upload4, upload5) fills the frame: the table or passage at least 90% of the
frame's width. On a board, zoom so the card being read fills most of the frame. When the camera
pushes onto a line of text, the whole line stays inside the frame; never cut a headline or a quoted
line at the frame's edge while it is on screen.

"EXPLAINER" cards are the film's own simple drawings in its type: a short definition and, where
the line describes one, a small diagram drawn on with arrows. Nothing drawn is a real object.

The section label at the top right changes at each heading below.
