# Chart data: Anthropic's IPO (film 31)

Every value a chart draws, with its unit, label and source. Nothing else may be drawn. Each chart is
**built on screen**: bars grow from zero in order, lines draw left to right, figures count up as the
narrator says them, and the frame drifts slowly while it holds. A source line sits under every chart.
Charts are drawn by the film (SK.bars, SK.chart, SK.counter, SK.stat); their numbers come from here.
