## The one thing the film must say first

Anthropic has **no public S-1 yet**. Every "prospectus" number comes from **drafts that reporters
were shown** (Reuters, 28 Sep and 20 Oct 2026; The Wall Street Journal, 20 Oct 2026). Anthropic did
not comment. Yahoo Finance (21 Oct): the public filing could come **"as soon as Friday"** (23 Oct),
three people familiar; a November listing is possible.

| adjusted profit | Anthropic told investors it had an **adjusted** operating profit in Q2 and Q3 2026; Q3 adjusted operating margin above 11%, positive cash flow | REPORTED | WSJ/CNBC (May), Bloomberg (Aug), FT (Oct) |
| what "adjusted" leaves out | stock-based pay; reports conflict on training costs. No GAAP operating result for 2026 has been reported | REPORTED | WSJ; R&D World; 31a |

How the charge works, in plain words: some backers lent money as notes that turn into shares later.
When Anthropic's price rises, the shares those notes will become are worth more, so what Anthropic
"owes" grows on paper, and accounting books the growth as a loss. No cash leaves. The better the
private rounds go, the bigger the paper loss. Amazon's 10-Q is the same notes seen from the other side.

## Unconfirmed / not known

- The public S-1, and every number in it. Drafts can change.
- A GAAP operating result for 2026; cash at 30 June 2026; stock-based pay.
- What "adjusted" excludes beyond stock-based pay (reports conflict on training costs).
- Whether the IPO price will be $2T, $1.5T or something else; how many shares it adds.
- Who the two biggest customers are.
- The "$8.7B real loss" is commenters' subtraction (80.7 - 72), not a reported operating figure.

## Commonly misreported

1. "Anthropic lost $80 billion": ~$72B of it is a non-cash charge that grows because the price rose.
2. "Run-rate" is not revenue: $47B and ~$65B are one month x 12.
3. "Anthropic is profitable": only on an adjusted basis, with an unclear definition.
4. "$750B committed": over $600B is committed; up to $150B more is still being negotiated.
5. "The S-1 shows": there is no public S-1 yet.
6. Cisco "never recovered": it did, in December 2025, 25.7 years later.

## The film must not say

- That Anthropic "lost $80 billion" without saying in the same breath that ~$72B is not cash.
- That Anthropic is or is not profitable, without "adjusted" or "GAAP".
- That $750B is committed; that the IPO is priced; that the filing is public.
- Who the two big customers are; any guess at them.
- Any politician's name, party or political judgement; the Pentagon dispute is told only as two court results.
- That AI "will" end humanity: only that the draft lists catastrophic or existential risks.
- Any number not on this sheet or the chart data file; any rounding into a bigger claim.
