# How good videos open: six measured on 2026-10-13

The first 45 seconds of six videos, each among the most watched of a channel that is good at what
our films try to do, put through `scripts/review-pack.py` and read frame by frame. Third-party
video is never committed; this is what was measured and seen. Six is a small sample: background
for an opinion, never a rule and never an ideal (a film is not wrong for being unlike them). Add
a row when a new kind of film needs one.

## What they share

- **Sound from the first or second second,** at -9 to -15 LUFS. None opens on silence or on
  music alone for more than a second.
- **The viewer is told what they will get by 14 s at the latest,** most by 6 s: three ticks, a
  lower third, a typed title, a banner. The one that waits 33 s for its title fills every second
  before it with motion.
- **The first picture is a person or a figure, large** (45 to 90 per cent of the frame's height),
  or the title itself. Never a small diagram.
- **The two padel videos play the shot inside the first 12 seconds.** The lesson comes after the
  viewer has seen what it is about.
- **A diagram, when it comes, is the whole frame** and is drawn on while it is explained.

## Where they differ, so do not make rules of these

- **Cutting.** Two of the six have no hard cut in 30 s and one has eleven. A calm film is not a
  slow one: Tifo's is still for 83% of its time by the stillness measure and is the most watched
  of its kind. What it never does is hold a finished picture: something is always being added.
- **Intros.** Tifo's type-only 20 s would be called slow on another channel. It is theirs, and
  their viewers came for it.
