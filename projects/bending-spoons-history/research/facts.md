## 1. Origins (2010–2014)

**Founders. Five are confirmed: Luca Ferrari, Francesco Patarnello, Matteo Danieli, Luca Querella and Tomasz Greber.**
- Ferrari, Patarnello and Danieli are the three who started Evertale. MEASURED, [P] team letter.
- They "use the leftover $40,000 to start a new company alongside Luca Querella and Tomasz Greber, two Evertale standouts." MEASURED, [P] team letter.
- In 2019 the company described its founders as "four Italian engineers and a polish designer". CLAIM, from its 2019 press release:
  https://api.elite-network.com/api/resources/download/news/bending-spoons-welcomes-h14-nuo-capital-and-startip-tip-group-as-shareholders/documents/bsp-nuovi-soci-eng-pdf
  - So Greber is the Polish designer. That is inferred from the release, which names no one.
- Ferrari, Patarnello, Danieli and Querella hold all the class A shares, at five votes each. After the IPO that gave them **82.71% of the voting power**. MEASURED, [P] cover page.
- Greber is not among the controlling holders. His current role was not found.

**How they met.** It was university, not McKinsey.
- All three Evertale founders hold engineering degrees from the **University of Padua** (Italy) and MSc degrees in Telecommunications Engineering from the **Technical University of Denmark (DTU)**. MEASURED, [P] management biographies.
- **Only Ferrari worked at McKinsey**, as an associate from 2010 to 2012. MEASURED, [P].
- The decision to start a company came on the night of **2 August 2010 in Lombok, Indonesia**, on a backpacking trip "to mark our graduation in engineering." MEASURED, [P] team letter.

**Evertale**
- It was "up and running" two months later, around October 2010. It was "a smartphone app that uses AI to automatically generate a user's diary."
- They "raise a million dollars, recruit a scrappy team of ten."
- "By mid-2013, we have essentially no revenue, barely four months of runway in the bank … Evertale is a failure."
- All of the above is MEASURED, [P] team letter.
- Roles at Evertale were Patarnello CEO (2010–13), Danieli CTO (2011–13) and Ferrari co-founder (2010–13). MEASURED, [P] biographies.
- The prospectus does not name Evertale's city. Copenhagen is implied, since Bending Spoons ApS was founded there, but it is not stated.

**The "$40,000"**
- It is what was left after Evertale was liquidated: "We liquidate Evertale and use the leftover $40,000 to start a new company." MEASURED, [P].
- The letter also says: "We were about to attempt to create a world-class company with $40,000, a team of five, and a track record that read 0 for 1." [P]
- And: "When we started Bending Spoons, we had around $40,000 in seed capital and paid **$10,000 for our first acquisition**." MEASURED, [P] p. 1 summary.

**Founding**
- "Bending Spoons ApS was founded in 2013 in Copenhagen, Denmark." MEASURED, [P] corporate history.
- The founders' board terms all start in **June 2013**, so the founding month is June 2013. MEASURED, [P].
- **The name** is "a nod to the fictional concept of bending spoons with the mind". It is a metaphor for attempting the impossible, and they "liked the silliness" of it. MEASURED, [P].
  - Reuters says the name was inspired by the spoon scene in *The Matrix*. REPORTED, Reuters 2024-10-21, as cited by Wikipedia: https://www.reuters.com/technology/italian-tech-company-bending-spoons-has-eye-us-potential-ipo-2024-10-21/
  - The prospectus does not mention The Matrix.

**Move to Milan. The two sources give different years:**

| Source | Says |
|---|---|
| 2019 company press release (CLAIM) | founded in Copenhagen in 2013 "and moved to Italy one year later", which is **2014** |
| Prospectus (MEASURED) | headquarters relocated "through a multi-step cross-border merger" in which the Italian S.r.l. "became the surviving entity in **2015**"; it became an S.p.A. in 2017 |

- Safe line for the film: "a year later, they moved to Milan". The legal move completed in 2015.
- Headquarters today: Via Nino Bonnet 10, Milan. MEASURED, [P].

## 2. The app years (2013–2021)

**Scale in 2018–2019.** CLAIM, from the 2019 press release above:
- 2018 turnover of **€45 million**, "with three-digit year-over-year growth".
- **200 million downloads** to date, growing by **200,000 per day on iOS**.

  - No download or revenue figure for either was found.

  - It was an asset deal and no team came with it. About 30 Spooners were assigned at first.

- Their role models are named in [P]: Henry Singleton (Teledyne), Tom Murphy (Capital Cities), Broadcom, Danaher and TransDigm.

## 6. The playbook and its reputation

**In their own words**
- "We intend to hold and operate the businesses we acquire indefinitely … we largely ignore exit value." MEASURED, [P].
- They decide prices "assuming we'll never sell the acquired business." [P]
- Their boilerplate on every 2026 release: "has never sold a material business."
- The transformation "typically … entails reorganizing teams, overhauling technology, redesigning user interfaces, accelerating product development, and enhancing marketing and monetization." MEASURED, every 6-K boilerplate.

**Layoffs, measured.** [P] says of AOL, Eventbrite and Vimeo: "In Q4 2025 and Q1 2026, we added 1,830 full-time equivalent team members through the acquisitions of AOL, Eventbrite, and Vimeo … once the transformations … are substantially complete later in 2026, we expect only a few hundred to remain." MEASURED.

**The criticism, reported**
- The repeated deep cuts:
  - Evernote: 129 people, then most of the US staff.
  - Filmic: all 22 staff.
  - WeTransfer: 75%.
  - komoot: about 80–85%.
  - Vimeo: "most" staff.
  - Airtable: a "large portion".
- Free tiers were narrowed: Evernote to 50 notes, WeTransfer to 10 transfers a month (Dec 2024, per TechCrunch's explainer).
- Komoot users complained about the paywall. BikeRadar and Escape Collective both covered it: https://escapecollective.com/how-komoot-lost-its-way
- The explainer is here: https://techcrunch.com/2026/01/25/what-is-bending-spoons-everything-to-know-about-aols-acquirer/ (read via a reader mirror)

**Their defence**
- They say they are not private equity because they never sell. TechCrunch, citing Ferrari on the 20VC podcast.
- Severance is generous: 16 weeks for Evernote US staff, and "substantial separation packages" at Airtable.
- They say smaller teams ship faster. Evernote releases rose 50% in 2023 and doubled in 2024 (CLAIM, [P]). The Airtable post says "smaller, faster-moving teams … can have an outsized impact".

## 10. Commonly misreported

- **Exchange.** It is Nasdaq (BSP), not NYSE. Some outlets wrote NYSE.
- **The October 2026 bond.** It was announced as $1.5B but **priced at about $1.8B** ($1.25B + €500M).
- **Berkshire's "4%"**:
  - The 6-K says it is 4% of the **ordinary** shares (346.5M). It is not 4% of all 656.7M shares.
  - $600M at the $29 IPO price would be about 20.7M shares, and with the new 10M that makes about 30.7M. That is about 8.9% of the ordinary class or 4.7% of all shares.
  - These numbers do not reconcile with "4% of ordinary". Berkshire may have bought above $29, or sold some shares. The company never disclosed the July share count.
  - **Say "about 4%" and do not compute it.**
- **Who raised the IPO money.** $1.68B is the whole deal. The company itself got about $1.0B gross and $1.10B net including the over-allotment option. The rest went to selling shareholders.
- **Filmic dates.** It was acquired in **2022**, not 2023. The staff were laid off in Nov/Dec 2023.
- **Vimeo dates.** $1.38B was the September 2025 **announcement**. It closed on **24 Nov 2025**.
- **AOL.** It was announced in October 2025 and closed on **2 Jan 2026**. The "$1.5B" was never official; the filing shows $1,454M.
- **Eventbrite.** It was announced in Dec 2025 and closed on **10 Mar 2026**.
- **Airtable and Miro.** Both are 2026 deals and both are confirmed. Airtable was the first deal after the IPO.
- **"Not profitable."**
  - FY2025 was a $0.2M net loss.
  - The trailing twelve months to March 2026 showed an $84.5M loss, which is how some outlets reported it.
  - But Q1 and Q2 2026 were profitable ($27.5M and $177.0M).
- **Headcount "under 1,000"** (Fortune). That fits the core Spooners (621). Total full-time-equivalent staff was 2,284 at the end of Q1 2026.
- **Paying customers.** "Ten million paying customers" (Verdict, Nov 2025) conflicts with the filing's 8M in Dec 2025 and 9M+ in March 2026. Use the filing.
- **Founding.** It was not "three founders". Three started Evertale, and **five** founded Bending Spoons.
- **McKinsey.** Only Ferrari worked there. They met through university, not McKinsey.
- **Milan.** Some sources say they moved in 2014 and the filing says 2015. See §1.
- **Wikipedia's MileIQ "$233 million".** That is almost certainly confused with Brightcove's $233M. MileIQ's price was never disclosed.
