# Script, word for word

## 1 · THE HOOK

- L01. *View `la`, all three layers on, camera drifting; headline "LA IS SITTING ON 24,333 OIL WELLS" (24,333 in amber), counter 24,333.*
  "Los Angeles County is sitting on more than twenty-four thousand oil wells. This is every one on the state's records."
- L02. *The plugged layer pulses while the others dim; "17,763 PLUGGED" counts up.*
  "Almost eighteen thousand are plugged: sealed with cement and left in the ground."
- L03. *Fast push into Echo Park / Westlake, flash cut to `westlake`.*
  "But look just west of downtown."

## 2 · THE OLD FIELD

- L04. *`westlake` aerial with no dots, then the wells layer sweeps on across the homes.*
  "This stripe is the Los Angeles City oil field, discovered in 1893."
- L05. *Camera travels along the band; "500 WELLS IN 4 YEARS".*
  "Within four years it had five hundred wells. Today, homes and apartments sit on top."
- L06. *"1,192 WELLS" counts up, then "856 STILL LISTED AS IDLE".*
  "The state lists nearly twelve hundred wells here. Eight hundred fifty-six are still listed as idle, not plugged."
- L07. *Big "900" in amber, "ORPHAN WELLS · CITY OF LA · STATE ESTIMATE".*
  "The state estimates nine hundred orphan wells in the city of Los Angeles, most of them buried."
- L08. *The city's quote set large, "only approximate" underlined as it is said.*
  "And the city says buried wells were not sealed to today's standards, and their mapped locations are sometimes only approximate."
- L09. *Ring two dots in the band; "ECHO PARK · 2016".*
  "In 2016, two of them were seeping gas in the front yards of two homes in Echo Park."

## 3 · HIDDEN IN PLAIN SIGHT

- L10. *Zoom out to `la`, push into Beverly Hills High School, cut to `bhhs`, ring the cluster: "19 WELLS".*
  "The wells that kept pumping learned to hide. Beverly Hills High School had nineteen behind a derrick painted with flowers."
- L11. *`thums`, slow push onto Island White; labels GRISSOM, WHITE, CHAFFEE, FREEMAN.*
  "And these islands off Long Beach, with palm trees and waterfalls? Oil islands, named after astronauts."

## 4 · NOW

- L12. *Back to `la`, all layers on, slow pull-out; "PHASE-OUT · 20 YEARS".*
  "In October, Los Angeles voted again to phase out oil drilling in the city within twenty years."
- L13. *Camera drifts over the dots; "CALGEM WELL FINDER" appears; hold on the glowing map, moving.*
  "So, is there one under your street? The state's Well Finder map shows every recorded well by address. Look yours up, and tell us what you found."
