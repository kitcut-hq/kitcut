# Every earthquake in California since 1990

The one thing the film says first: the USGS catalog holds more than forty-seven thousand magnitude 2.5+ earthquakes in and just off California since 1 January 1990, and on one map they trace the San Andreas fault by themselves.

## Unconfirmed

- The 30-year odds are a forecast from 2014, not a prediction of a date.

## Commonly misreported

- "California will fall into the ocean": the San Andreas slides sideways; it does not pull apart (Earthquake Country Alliance).
- "The San Andreas is overdue": scientists say "late in the cycle", not overdue (SCEC #6291).
- "Earthquakes are increasing": SCSN says the M3.3+ rate has been about constant since 1932. Our decade layers differ because of big sequences (Landers 1992, Ridgecrest 2019), not a trend.

## The film must not say

- "overdue"
- "the next 30 years" without "from 2014"
- "the Big One is coming"
- "fall into the ocean"
- "increasing"
