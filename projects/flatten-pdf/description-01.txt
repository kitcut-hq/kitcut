Flattening a PDF turns a fillable form into a plain one. Every form field is merged into the page it sits on — the boxes, checkboxes and dropdowns stop being editable, whatever they were showing is printed onto the page, and the text stays selectable. Nothing moves and nothing is redrawn.

▶ Free Flatten PDF tool: https://instafill.ai/tools/flatten-pdf
No watermark, no hidden costs, unlimited files up to 10 MB each.

WHEN FLATTENING HELPS
• Lock answers into a form before you send it out
• Turn a packet assembled from several sources into one consistent document
• Stop a mix of fillable and flat pages from confusing automatic field detection
• Keep the layout untouched and the text selectable
