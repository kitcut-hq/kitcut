Flattening a PDF turns a fillable form into a plain one. The boxes, checkboxes and dropdowns stop being editable, and whatever they were showing is printed onto the page instead. Nothing moves, nothing is redrawn, and the text stays selectable.

This video walks through the case where that is genuinely useful: a document packet made of several forms, where some pages already carry fillable fields and others are completely flat. Because the packet already contains fields, automatic flat-to-fillable conversion can skip the flat pages. Flatten the whole packet first and you get one consistent document — then Instafill can make all of it fillable in one pass.
